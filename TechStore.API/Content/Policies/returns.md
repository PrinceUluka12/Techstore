We don't accept returns or exchanges, including for a change of mind. Instead, you check your phone before you accept it, and anything wrong is sorted out there and then.

## Check before you accept

When you collect from a shop, check the order with our staff before you leave. When it's delivered, open and check it in front of the delivery rider before you accept it. Make sure that:

- it's the model, colour and storage size you ordered;
- the box and phone aren't damaged, and everything listed on the box is inside;
- the phone switches on and works.

## If something is wrong

Don't accept it. Tell the shop staff or the rider, and contact us straight away on [support@hytelphones.ng](mailto:support@hytelphones.ng) or WhatsApp. We'll replace it with the right item or refund you in full.

## Once you've accepted it

Once you've accepted the order, it's yours. We can't take it back or swap it. If a fault appears later and the product page lists a warranty, the manufacturer's service centre handles it, and we'll help you find the right one.

## Refunds

We refund in full, including any delivery fee, when:

- we cancel an order you've paid for, for example because the item is no longer available;
- you refuse an order at delivery or pickup because it's wrong or faulty.

Refunds go back by bank transfer. We'll contact you to confirm the account to send it to. An order that's cancelled because it wasn't paid for within 24 hours has nothing to refund.

## Your rights

This policy doesn't affect your rights under Nigerian law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act.
