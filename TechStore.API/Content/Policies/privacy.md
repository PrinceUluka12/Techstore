This policy explains what personal information Hytel Phones Ltd collects through this website, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## What we collect

- **Your account, if you make one:** name, email address and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed), so no one can read it, including us.
- **Ordering without an account:** your name, email address and phone number, kept with the order.
- **Your orders:** phone number, delivery address or chosen pickup shop, what you bought, coupons used and payment references.
- **What you add:** reviews, your wishlist and your cart.
- **Our newsletter, if you sign up:** your email address.
- **Security:** your IP address, used to limit repeated sign-in attempts.

We don't collect card details. Payments are made by bank transfer from your own bank.

## Why we use it

- to process, deliver and keep you updated on your orders, including by email;
- to run your account, including password resets;
- to keep the site and your account secure and prevent fraud;
- to keep the sales records the law requires.

We don't sell your information. We only send our newsletter if you sign up for it, and every one has a link to unsubscribe.

## Who we share it with

- **Delivery partners:** your name, phone number and address, only to deliver your order.
- **Microsoft Azure:** hosts this website, its database and the emails it sends. The servers are outside Nigeria. Microsoft protects the data under its own security and privacy commitments.
- **A mailing service,** if you sign up for the newsletter: your email address, only to send it.
- **Authorities:** only when the law requires it.

## Cookies and storage in your browser

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in. Your browser also stores your sign-in details and the products you viewed recently. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. The site's fonts load from Google, which sees your IP address when they do.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. Order records are kept for as long as the law requires for accounting and tax, even after an account is deleted. A newsletter sign-up stays on the list until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
- correct anything that's wrong;
- delete your information, unless we have to keep it by law;
- stop or limit how we use it.

Email [support@hytelphones.ng](mailto:support@hytelphones.ng) and we'll reply as quickly as we can, within the time the law allows. If you're not happy with our reply, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Keeping it safe

The site only works over an encrypted connection. Staff only see the information they need for their job. If a breach ever affected your information, we'd tell you and the Commission as the law requires.

## Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes, and the date at the top will show when.
